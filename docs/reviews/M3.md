## What changed since M2

Seating each letter on the ribbon bend cleared the 15 pairs and made the names unreadable in the classical and medieval frames, so that drawing path is not kept. The shipped pin keeps one straight column, or one horizontal line, on the centerline at the middle of the name, and slides the name along its own ribbon when the boxes still meet. Text, font size, and fit are unchanged. Hover-only names are unchanged. The 21 shortened fitted names were already shortened by `labelFit` before the pin.

## Fix list

- Item 8 stays a fail while nine names are hover-only, 21 fitted names are not the full polity name, and callout leaders are not painted. Zero ink hits is the collision half of the item.
- Item 2 stays partial while Rome is one ribbon, the Carolingian children `east_francia` and `west_francia` are culled, no merge relation exists, and `chalukya` jumps 96px in 0.62px of y. Do not invent a Western or Eastern Roman polity, and do not invent a merge.
- Sixty separate bottom-edge labels need the crowded slices to keep more states that already exist.
- A twelfth cross-cutting band needs a real sourced event.
- Images and painted callouts stay P4.

## Decision

M3 is 5/11. Stay in layout tuning. The next lever is the rivers: keep existing split children drawn when that is what routes a fork, and stop post-drift packing from breaking the drift cap. Do not open P4. Do not open P6. P8 stays off.
