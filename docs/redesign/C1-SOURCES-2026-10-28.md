## What the diagrams represent

Three original SVGs and WebP thumbnails are generated by `scripts/redesign/make-early-cities-diagrams.mjs`. They are released locally as **explanatory diagrams**, a distinct media type. Their creation date is 28 October 2026; represented ancient dates are separate. No external historical figure, photograph or copyrighted plan was traced or reproduced. Diagram text, captions and alt text preserve the conventions.

- **Uruk:** categories, quantities and the durable record are analytical tasks. No ancient signs, quantities, individuals or full translation are invented. The actual tablet photograph remains available.
- **Dholavira:** well/drain and East/South reservoirs are component boxes in schematic relative orientation. The dashed box identifies the study area east of the East Reservoir, not hypothetical reservoir outlines. There is no scale, measured boundary, connecting pipe, flow arrow or claim that all features operated at once. This is a component guide, not the promised future fully phased archaeological plan.
- **Liangzhu:** construction materials, management of water, production and movement are a concept sequence. It is not a dam cross-section, canal route, map of rice fields or reconstruction of one working day. The original cong photograph remains available.

Original diagram artwork uses CC BY 4.0, with credit and licence links in the reader, inspection dialog and print. Existing Met images retain their existing CC0/public-domain credits and original bytes. The new diagrams are not additional historical photographs. No generative historical illustration was used.

## Chronology and inference

All 77 existing dossier IDs, intervals and interval explanations remain intact. Uruk’s c3100–2900 BCE object case now points to a separate c3300–2350 BCE teaching context. The latter spans selected urban development and Early Dynastic orientation; it is not Uruk’s lifetime. Dholavira retains c3000–1500 BCE while exposing the GPR introduction’s c1700 endpoint. Its broad phase labels and the regional seal window do not date every water structure. Liangzhu retains the c3300–2300 site frame, the broader Met culture label and the individual object date.

Liangzhu’s rounded BCE dam orientations are explicit derivations from the paper’s calibrated BP clusters using 1950 as present: 5200–4800 cal BP becomes approximately 3250–2850 BCE; 5000–4800 becomes approximately 3050–2850 BCE. They are not new calibrated estimates or exact building boundaries. The reference frame remains within the atlas’s 3300 BCE lower bound.

The new path follows thematic order: Uruk, Dholavira, Liangzhu. Its route years are viewing choices within existing cases, not contemporaneous snapshots or a causal sequence. No new historical relationship is inferred from overlap, similarity or path membership.

## Research still needed

Household life is brought into the questions and connected to production, but detailed household, gender, childhood, recruitment, coercion and distribution histories are not established by these sources. Uruk needs dated fourth-millennium domestic assemblages and more local scholarship. Dholavira needs full visual/phase inspection of the ASI plans and excavation reports, plus stronger evidence of access and maintenance. Liangzhu needs inspection of original figures/supplement and wider archaeological literature on labor, households, ritual and environmental change. No single environmental cause for abandonment is introduced.

Independent review should assess whether the explanations make the mechanisms clear without overstating what the sources establish. A real adult/family session should test the path and evidence distinctions. Those are separate from author self-review and browser/PDF checks.
