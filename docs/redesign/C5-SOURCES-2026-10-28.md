No analytical result transferred to Met 1977.126. Figures not reproduced or used as surveyed maps.
- Met Abbasid essay and bowl 1977.126: institutional record read through browser-tool text. 762 foundation, 836–892 capital phase, 945/1055 changes and 1258 sack remain separate.
