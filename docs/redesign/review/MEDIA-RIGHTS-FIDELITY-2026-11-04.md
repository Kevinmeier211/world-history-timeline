## Results

Rights evidence supports 93 records, qualifies 10 and leaves reproduction of two archive scans unresolved. The atlas supplies original reference cards and external links for both scans. Fidelity is supported for 104 records. The Ocmulgee figure preserves the source pixels, but its published descriptions disagree about panel C.

For all 44 Met objects, the current official API identified the accession and its public-domain image status. The 57 adopted museum views were compared with the official master images across 158 display, alternate, thumbnail and print files. The largest mean RGB difference was 3.324 on a 0–255 scale after downsampling. All 22 authored SVGs and their thumbnails regenerated byte-for-byte. The two external-image reports identify the comparison sources, frames and available date evidence individually.

- Corrected Dholavira diagram alt text, Kerma and Nimrud descriptions, and four diagram citation targets. The drawing licences and historical evidence now have separate links.
- Separated the Sanam shabti’s workshop findspot from the mould’s temple context. The Mound City plan now distinguishes the 1846 survey drawing from its 1848 publication. The Ocmulgee credit distinguishes the published composite from separate reuse of its components.
- Updated the source-specific rights wording for Hine, the war poster, the Wendat radiocarbon figure and the Indonesian proclamation.
