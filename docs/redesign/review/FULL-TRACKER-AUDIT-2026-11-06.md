No supported corrected version was verified; the Playwright issue's closed label is not proof of a shipped engine fix.

**Endpoint:** identify a supported corrected build, rerun the app-free replacement control and both application navigation probes with strict zero-page-error requirements, and rerun affected candidate/browser checks. Preserve failed evidence and unchanged timing limits. Do not suppress matching messages or loosen browser security. Any runtime/dependency change requires its own affected verification.
