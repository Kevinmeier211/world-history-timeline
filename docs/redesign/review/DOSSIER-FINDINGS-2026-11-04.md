## Historical uncertainties to preserve

Some questions require evidence of a different kind. Wider reading of the same source cannot recover an unnamed artisan from a stylistic attribution, a complete itinerary from residue, every household’s diet from a sampled assemblage, or a community’s experience from a ruler’s announcement. Hattusha’s tree-ring chronology and the two published Wendat date-probability descriptions retain uncertainty about an exact event year. Comparisons involving Hopeton, Chaco, Cahokia, Ozette and Ocmulgee retain the distinct dates of archaeology, later photography, restoration and living community testimony.
