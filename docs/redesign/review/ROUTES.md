# Route and comparison review inputs

This register checks structure and makes ordering explicit. It does not establish that a comparison is historically justified or that a reader has learned the intended concept.

All 33 current journeys have been inventoried with exact stop subjects, dates, regions and comparison targets. All 77 dossiers have an authored comparison partner, and all 76 authored pairs have at least one shared dimension. These facts remove structural dead ends; source asymmetry and interpretation still require review.

The twelve theme groups in `src/atlas/learning-themes.json` route readers to existing investigations. The childhood, migration, medicine and war/resistance groups explicitly state their limits. A theme containing three journeys does not satisfy C08 until the substantive stops and source-supported transitions have been reviewed.

Before acceptance, check each stop’s relationship to its case dates, the transition question, whether a comparison is grounded in both sources, how the final response uses evidence, and whether the next/previous/exit/resume actions preserve the intended context. Durations remain estimates until observed.
