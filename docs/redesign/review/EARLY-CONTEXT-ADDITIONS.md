The preservation tests compare all previous sources/contexts and all 77 subjects. Only the two named contextual links and two chapter coverage explanations may differ among existing records; original case intervals, objects, journeys, comparisons and glossary remain unchanged.

These additions advance C1-R7, C2-R3 and selected snapshot entries. They do not close the 56-orientation or 84-entry program. C1 dates earlier than the supported Kuk range, a mainland Southeast Asian orientation, full C2 European chapter coverage, and the remaining regional review are still open.
