## Claims deliberately excluded or qualified

The Met chronology incorrectly associates Huizong’s northern loss with Mongols. That sentence is excluded; its separate Jurchen/Jin and 1127 entry supports the phase distinction. The 1069 memorial’s explicit date is used without importing its introductory appointment chronology. Neither exceptionalist prosperity rankings nor traffic/production/treasury totals are adopted. Marco Polo’s Yuan passage is not Song traffic data.

The Sahel exhibition uses 1324 for Musa’s passage via Cairo, while the later article says 1325 for his pilgrimage. The original 1324 Cairo anchor remains and the wording difference is disclosed. The approximate Mali frame is not an exact founding/extinction date. A European image’s wealth claim is not an audited fortune, observed portrait or certain political boundary. Archaeology at one settlement does not establish uniform administration everywhere.

The tunic’s photographed orientation and the catalogue’s upper/lower terminology are not collapsed into one visual assertion. Its updated alt text describes the supplied image; the account names construction features without imposing that orientation. No worker identity, state commission, findspot, route or labor category is invented. Rotating service, specialist work and resettlement are not interchangeable. Provisioning is not proof of universal welfare, consent or equal returns.
