## Images, geography and rights

One new atlas media record reproduces **Figure 4, coins from Beta Samati**, credited to **I. Dumitru**, in Harrower et al. The publisher explicitly licenses the article under [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/); the figure has no separate third-party credit. Five columns show two views of each coin. The source identifies, left to right, Armah, Armah, an anonymous ruler, pre-Christian Ezana and MHDYS. This is a modern excavation figure published in 2019, with separately represented fourth–seventh-century material and qualified Armah chronology. It is not five issues of 200 CE or a life-size reproduction.

The exact publisher image was downloaded and inspected before conversion. `make-exchange-media.mjs` performs resizing without enlargement and WebP encoding only: no crop, retouching, new labels or reconstructed detail. Source dimensions are 1583×919. Full credit/licence reach online, inspection and print; the original JPEG stays in research. Figure 3's plan/photograph was inspected privately and is not reproduced. Existing Met images keep their bytes and gain explicit CC0 links where used.

Three original geographic studies use approximate locators. Palmyra's selected exchange sequence is explicitly schematic, not an excavated road or this family's itinerary. The Kushan map marks Peshawar, Mathura and a **broad Baghlan temple-inscription region**, avoiding a false exact point for Rabatak/Surkh Kotal or the coin. The Aksum map separates highland city and coastal Adulis; a **rounded Yeha-district research locator** is not the exact Beta Samati excavation coordinate. No new imperial polygon or historical relation is inferred from those points or the learning path.

All original case IDs, intervals, notes and alternatives survive. Reference windows are teaching frames, not founding/extinction dates. The new inquiry crosses dates deliberately and extends beyond C4's 500 CE chapter edge without changing that edge. More original-language/legal/numismatic, neighborhood, agricultural, gender, enslavement and regional work remains. Independent source/explanation review and real adult/family, screen-reader, physical-device and physical-print acceptance are separate tasks.
