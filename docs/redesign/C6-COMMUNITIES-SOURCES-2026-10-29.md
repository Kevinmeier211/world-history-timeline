The file record marks the **underlying 1850 work Public Domain** and also reproduces provider **CC BY-NC-SA 3.0** wording. `chaco-rumsey-rights`, the actual [Rumsey Copyright and Permissions page](https://www.davidrumsey.com/about/copyright-and-permissions), links that restrictive licence while also describing broader publication uses, including for-profit publishers. This does not relabel the scan CC0, establish universal commercial permission or turn it into new Chronos artwork. A source-faithful copy retains the original bounds; the caption supplies the principal reading of small labels.

New PMC isotope-paper and USGS fact-sheet attempts were challenged/403; a 2012 agriculture paper returned 403 and the Chaco Research Archive page returned 403. Search-index leads are not full article/database reads. The archive’s malformed indexed excavation date is not silently adopted. ## Remaining limits

The source packets distinguish full short pages, selected research passages, abstracts, institutional excerpts, image pixels and access failures. An accessible article is not automatically figure permission; a living community account is attributed evidence, not consent. This bounded work does not resolve all governance, household inequality, labor, food, environmental or descendant-history questions.

Independent historian/archaeologist/conservator and community review, a real adult/family session, human screen-reader behavior, physical-device behavior and physical print quality remain open. Tagged PDFs would not establish PDF/UA conformance. C6 adoption and candidate verification must be assessed from their own actual receipts; no passing C5 result is relabelled as C6 evidence.
