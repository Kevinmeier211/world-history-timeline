## Open and investigate

The local preview is http:// 127.0.0.1:4174/. For a fresh portable launch, extract `output/redesign/release/chronos-local-rc.zip`, enter its folder and run `python3 launch.py`. Source development instructions are in the root README.

1. **Overall atlas:** Full history presents eight chronological inquiry frames, from 3300 BCE through 2000 CE. Choose a chapter, browse a geographic scope, and move between readable time bars and world-at-time locators. Equal overview rows are reading frames, not equal durations.
2. **A complete journey:** Open Journeys → **The world around 200 CE**. Inspect the Han watchtower, follow the sourced Palmyra network with intermediaries, investigate Aksum and Teotihuacan, then compare the Colima and Han models. Answer the evidence question and save a return prompt. Sources, detours and exit preserve coherent navigation.
3. **Comparison:** Search **Nimrud**, choose Compare, then select **Ashoka**. Read the aligned authority/evidence dimensions, their precise citations and the limits of comparison. The view establishes no contact merely from similarity.
4. **Adult depth:** **Hattusha** separates a dated drought from an unproved causal explanation. **Qin** juxtaposes imperial standards with primary research on everyday local practice. **Te Tiriti** compares consequential differences between texts. **1947** distinguishes independence, a border announcement and personal disruption. Claims retain uncertainty and agent review labels.
5. **Revisit:** Save a question or journey in Notebook, hide/reveal earlier thinking, export JSON and import it in another browser. No account or remote transmission is required.

## Genuine limitations

This is an intentionally selective atlas, not an exhaustive catalogue. Some cells are limited or unrepresented and are named explicitly; every macro-region nevertheless receives substantive sourced treatment across the release. Maps are broad locators with orientation coastlines, not reconstructed political borders or exact object itineraries. Source selection reflects what was consulted, and institutional accounts themselves can disagree.

No independent historian approval, family test, human screen-reader session, physical tablet/phone Safari test or physical print inspection occurred. Chromium emulation, keyboard testing, semantic inspection, automated accessibility and rendered PDFs establish the recorded facts only. Future public hosting must reproduce compression/static routing conventions; the measurements describe the packaged local server, not a deployed site. Notebook data belongs to browser storage: export remains the way to preserve or move it.
