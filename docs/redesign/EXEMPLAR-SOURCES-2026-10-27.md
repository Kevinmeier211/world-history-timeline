The historical images were visually inspected before conversion. WebP resizing/encoding changes delivery format, not historical features; no synthetic evidence was created. Source hashes are in `output/implementation-2026-10-27/research/image-receipts.json`. All local derivatives are checked by `verify-media.mjs` for decodability and declared dimensions.

[The National Archives copyright terms](https://www.nationalarchives.gov.uk/terms-and-conditions/copyright/) and the image-specific record distinguish Crown text reuse under OGL from image reproduction arrangements. The images are therefore retained only within this explicitly local educational candidate; do not carry them into a public release without resolving reproduction or replacing them with permitted alternatives. The public-page content is evidence, not publication authorization.
