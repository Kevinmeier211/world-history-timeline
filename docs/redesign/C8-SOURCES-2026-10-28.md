Consulted 28 October 2026 UTC; work began on the evening of 27 October in Denver. This is source consultation and self-review by Codex, not independent historian approval. The local workpack adds Indonesia and Ghana and expands the existing South Africa case. It connects them to the already expanded Partition investigation.

## Geography and date decisions

The three new local maps show selected approximate locators over the existing Natural Earth/world-atlas 110m coastline dataset. Ghana’s view is cropped to the southern places actually discussed. Local projection now uses the central latitude and a common scale for both axes; it no longer stretches each region independently to fill the frame. The coastline stroke stays thin when magnified. Geometry remains coarse world-scale orientation; a higher-resolution regional map is still future work. The maps are not boundary reconstructions, measured travel routes or military-control maps. Numbered points have equivalent named controls and group when their touch targets overlap.

New case windows are Indonesia 1945–1950 and Ghana 1948–1960. Ghana’s separate reference frame extends to 1964 to show the one-party turn. South Africa keeps its original 1994–1997 case while adding a 1948–1997 reference frame. All other original case date explanations remain intact.

## Research that remains

Indonesia needs more Indonesian civilian and regional scholarship. Ghana needs named organizers, opponents and local economic/territorial histories. South Africa needs fuller resistance histories, other negotiating voices and evidence of outcomes. These are bounded accounts with substantial explanation, not definitive national histories. The 1950 world view remains selective, especially in East Asia, the Americas and broader Cold War institutions.

The earlier Partition/TNA reproduction-clearance limitation remains unresolved. No public publication or new portable package is claimed.
