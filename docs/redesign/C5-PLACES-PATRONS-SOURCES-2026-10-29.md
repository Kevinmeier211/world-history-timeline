## Consultation and acceptance limits

Selected museum scholarship, primary research, institutional pages, abstracts, indexed first-page text and actual image pixels have different evidentiary roles. Unavailable bibliography is not silently treated as read; a freely accessible PDF is not automatic illustration permission. There is no newly inferred ivory voyage, monolithic Maya state, ancient Borobudur itinerary or contact connecting the three cases.

Author-team source consultation and internal checks do not establish historical completeness, independent historian/archaeologist/epigrapher/conservator or community approval, a real adult/family session, human screen-reader behavior, physical-device behavior, physical print quality or PDF/UA conformance. These acceptance tasks remain separate and open. No push, deployment, publication, external message or automation is supplied by this workpack.
