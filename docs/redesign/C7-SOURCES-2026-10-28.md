## Voyage and Indian Ocean setting

The downloaded [1898 English edition](https://www.gutenberg.org/cache/epub/46440/pg46440-images.html) of the anonymous first-voyage journal was consulted at printed **pp. 39–57**, and the opening **pp. 1–2** were checked afterward for departure/editorial conventions. Ravenstein is translator/editor, not the eyewitness narrator. Traditional Álvaro Velho authorship is not asserted. The text explains that bracketed words/dates are editorial insertions. The selected passages cover capture before Malindi, later return of captives, meetings, detention of a royal servant while seeking a pilot, crossing, multilingual contacts and the Calicut audience. The selected pilot is unnamed: no Ibn Majid identity or inferred ethnicity/religion is supplied.

The narrator’s prejudiced descriptions, religious identifications, crowd estimates, crop names and measures are not adopted as neutral facts. The book’s later illustrations and editorial appendices do not become 1498 eyewitness images. No original Portuguese or complete critical edition is claimed. The compiled source scope records both selections; the departure point cites the opening page while retaining the editorial status of the Lisbon heading.
